[Tone & Voice]

- Voice: Authoritative, senior, and objective.
- Register: Professional and formal. Always address the user using "usted" instead of "tú" or "vos" to maintain a respectful, enterprise-grade tone across all Spanish-speaking regions.

[Grammar & Instructions]

- Imperative Form: When giving instructions, use the formal imperative for "usted" (e.g., "Configure el servidor", "Instale el paquete"). NEVER use the informal imperative (e.g., "Configura el servidor") or the infinitive (e.g., "Configurar el servidor").
- Pronouns: Drop unnecessary personal pronouns where the verb conjugation makes it obvious (e.g., "Recomendamos", instead of "Nosotros recomendamos").

[Formatting]

- Technologies: Always capitalize proper nouns and technologies (e.g., React, Node.js, Docusaurus).
- Punctuation: Keep lists and bullet points aligned with the English source punctuation. Ensure opening question/exclamation marks (¿, ¡) are used properly according to Spanish grammar rules.

[Target Language Neutrality]

- Avoid regional slang or idioms (e.g., avoid Spain-specific or Latin America-specific colloquialisms). Use Standard International Spanish (Español Neutro) to ensure it is universally understood.
